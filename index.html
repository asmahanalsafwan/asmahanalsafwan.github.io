About

I am an education researcher with a disciplinary foundation in physics and extensive experience teaching secondary physics. My academic research began in applied physics, nanotechnology and materials science before moving within Curtin into science education. My doctoral work examined Saudi students’ and teachers’ understandings of thermal concepts and the roles of language, culture and curriculum in conceptual change.

My current work extends this interest in learning and meaning-making into early childhood pedagogy, narrative inquiry and The Guardian Pedagogy, including Story Affordances, the Guardian Inquiry Cycle and Human Questions.

Current research

Story Affordances

Explores how educational possibilities emerge through the relationship among narrative features, children or groups, and context.

Guardian Inquiry Cycle

Models how narrative encounters can move through wonder, inquiry, intentional teaching, documentation and renewed story possibilities.

Human Questions

Explores questions of human meaning that remain recognisable while interpretations and answers remain open to change.

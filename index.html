About

I am an independent educational researcher and early childhood educator. My academic work began in physics and materials science before moving into science education, conceptual change and, more recently, early childhood curriculum and narrative pedagogy.

My current work develops The Guardian Pedagogy and related concepts, including Story Affordances, the Guardian Inquiry Cycle and Human Questions.

Central research question

How do Guardian Stories become enduring, renewable sources of learning?

Current research

Concept 01

Story Affordances

Examines how educational possibilities emerge through the relationship among narrative features, children or groups, and context.

Concept 02

Guardian Inquiry Cycle

Models how narrative encounters can move through wonder, inquiry, intentional teaching, documentation and renewed story possibilities.

Concept 03

Human Questions

Explores questions of human meaning that remain recognisable while interpretations and answers remain open to change.

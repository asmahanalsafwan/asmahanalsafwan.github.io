About

A research profile shaped by movement across disciplines rather than confinement to a single one.

I am an independent educational researcher and early childhood educator with an interdisciplinary academic background spanning physics, nanotechnology and materials science, science education, conceptual change, curriculum and narrative pedagogy.

My current work develops The Guardian Pedagogy, a narrative-based pedagogical framework concerned with how stories can become enduring, renewable sources of learning. The work explores story affordances, inquiry, human questions, documentation and the role of educators in recognising and extending learning generated through narrative.

Current research focus

Central proposition: How do Guardian Stories become enduring, renewable sources of learning?

Current conceptual work examines Story Affordances, the Guardian Inquiry Cycle and Human Questions in Narrative.

Current research programme

The Guardian Pedagogy is being developed as a connected programme of conceptual scholarship rather than a single isolated publication.

Concept 01

Story Affordances

Examines how educational possibilities emerge relationally among narrative features, children or groups, and the learning context.

Concept 02

Guardian Inquiry Cycle

Models how narrative encounters can move through wonder, inquiry, intentional teaching, documentation and renewed story possibilities.

Concept 03

Human Questions

Explores continuity-bearing questions of human meaning that remain recognisable while interpretations and answers remain open to change.
